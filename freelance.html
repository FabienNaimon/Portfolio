Pricing

Packs & Rates

All packages are modular and adaptable to your project's scope. Prices are indicative ranges — we'll define the exact scope together.

🕹️ Game Design

Pack 1 — Idea & Concept
150 – 200 €
Initial exchange (1h) to define your needs. Mechanic or original concept design. Design loop: objective, challenge, reward. Delivery: synthesis document (PDF or Notion).
Most popular
Pack 2 — System & Documentation
350 – 500 €
Includes Pack 1. Addition of one or two secondary gameplay systems (draft version: progression, economy, scoring…). Basic balancing tables. Detailed PDF or Notion report.
Pack 3 — Collaboration
700 – 900 € / month
Ongoing project audit. Design adjustment proposals. Regular feedback (weekly or on-demand). Collaborative work on Notion, Miro or Google Docs. Option: one-off intervention at 60€/h. Blueprint prototyping assistance.

🗻 Level Design

Pack 1 — Blockout & Intent
150 – 350 €
Layout creation (paper or engine). Clear gameplay intention. Rhythm, difficulty curve analysis. Synthetic presentation + delivery document (PDF or Notion).
Most popular
Pack 2 — Test & Iteration
350 – 450 €
Includes Pack 1. One iteration on the level based on feedback. Flow and navigation optimization. Detailed PDF or Notion report.
Pack 3 — Full Project
800 € / month
Design of multiple levels (duration defined by project scope). Unreal Engine integration with gameplay/technical constraints. Complete documentation. 1 to 3 month follow-up.

💻 QA Testing

Pack 1 — Flash Report
80 – 120 €
Quick test session (1–2h). Synthetic report of major bugs. UX / gameplay fluidity feedback.
  • + 50€: video report with commentary
  • + 20€/h: integration support (Unity/UE)
Most popular
Pack 2 — Complete Report
200 – 300 €
3 to 6h test session. Complete structured report (title, severity, reproduction, platform). UX / flow improvement suggestions.
  • + 30€: vocal debrief or coaching (30min–1h)
Pack 3 — Full Project QA
500 – 900 € / month
Regular build follow-up (scope-defined). Tests at each update. Ticket tracking & bug reproduction. Regression testing. Communication via Trello / Notion / Jira.
